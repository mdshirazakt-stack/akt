What is a dismissed impression? Your ad appears as a full-screen popup immediately after a user logs in. Because the ad blocks the interface, the user must actively see and close it to continue. Every user who closes it counts as one dismissed impression — a genuinely engaged view. Each user is shown your ad only once, ever. This guarantees zero repeat exposure to the same person.

How it works

  • Your ad appears as a full-screen overlay immediately after a verified member logs in.
  • Each member sees it exactly once — never again after closing.
  • You purchase a fixed number of dismissed impressions (minimum 100).
  • The campaign stops automatically when your target is reached.
  • Only one ad runs on the platform at any given time — no competition for attention.

Reporting & transparency

  • You receive a timestamped delivery log showing when each impression was shown and dismissed — no user names, emails, or phone numbers are ever shared.
  • You can see live impression counts at any time by contacting the admin.
  • Campaigns can be paused and resumed at admin discretion.
