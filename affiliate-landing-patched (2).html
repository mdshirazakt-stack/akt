Common questions

Everything you'd want to know

Is there a fee to join the program?

No. The telecrm Affiliate Program is free to join. There are no signup fees, no platform fees, and no hidden costs. You only get paid — never charged.

Do I need to be a CRM expert to sell telecrm?

Not at all. We provide the full sales pack — pitch deck, demo videos, ROI calculator, objection-handling guides. Our SDRs can also co-pitch with you on customer calls. If you can introduce a business to telecrm, that's enough.

How and when do I get paid?

Direct bank transfer, every month. There's no minimum threshold and no 90-day attribution windows. As soon as your customer pays telecrm, you earn 10% — paid out the following month.

What if my customer cancels?

You earn commission on every payment they make to telecrm. If they cancel, your earnings on that customer stop — but you keep everything you've already earned. There are no clawbacks.

Are there any quotas or minimum referrals?

No quotas, no minimums. Refer 1 customer or 100 — the program works the same way. There are also no exclusivity clauses; you're free to recommend other tools alongside telecrm.

Can I sell to clients I'm already consulting for?

Absolutely. In fact, that's our most successful partner archetype. If you're a sales consultant, marketing agency, or business advisor, telecrm slots naturally into your service offering — and you earn recurring commission on top of your existing fees.

How do I track my earnings?

You get a live affiliate dashboard the moment you're approved. Track every click, signup, paid customer, and rupee earned in real time. No spreadsheets, no monthly reports to chase.
