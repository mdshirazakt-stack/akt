Contribute to the Family Archive

Apno Ki Talash grows through careful community contribution. Every update should protect family dignity, improve accuracy, and avoid changes that create confusion in relationships or source history.

Moderator

Moderators help keep profile details accurate. They may review and update profile names, birth or death dates, root places, birth places, current places, and private contact fields such as email and mobile number when a profile claim or correction supports the change.

Admin

Admins handle higher-trust operations. They may review relationship structure, spouse or parent-child assignments, duplicate-profile linking, GEDCOM imports/exports, user access, and claim application where a mistake could affect several families.

What profile-detail work includes

  • Correcting spelling, honorifics, initials, and name ordering while preserving the person being represented.
  • Adding or improving date fields such as birth date, death date, or known date notes.
  • Updating locations such as root place, birth place, current place, or other profile-level place information.
  • Associating private contact details with the record after a profile claim. These contact details are for verification and administration, not public display.

What requires admin review

  • Adding, removing, or changing parent-child relationships.
  • Changing spouse links, marriage groups, or child assignment under a spouse.
  • Merging duplicate profiles or deciding which record should be treated as the primary profile.
  • Importing new GEDCOM files, deleting sources, or applying changes that alter a large part of the archive.

Other ways to contribute

People who maintain their own shajra, know families that are not yet represented here, or can identify incomplete branches are welcome to contribute further. The most useful contribution is genuine, correct, and traceable data: names, family links, places, dates, source context, and corrections that can be reviewed responsibly.

If you know a missing branch or have a structured family dataset, use the correction tools or apply for a role if you can help with ongoing review work.

Community badges

Active contributors are recognised with a badge visible on their profile and in search results. Badges are awarded by admins — they cannot be self-assigned or requested directly.

⭐ Contributor

Awarded to members who have actively contributed to the archive — by sharing corrections, providing missing family data, verifying records, or submitting accurate information that has been reviewed and applied by admins. This is the first badge most members can earn.

🛡️ Moderator

Awarded to trusted members who regularly help review and improve profile details. Moderators have a history of careful, accurate contributions and solid knowledge of the community. This badge reflects ongoing active participation, not a one-time action.

⚙️ Admin

Awarded to members who manage higher-trust operations such as relationship structures, GEDCOM imports, duplicate resolution, and user access. Admins have deep familiarity with the archive and are accountable to the site owners for the changes they apply.

👑 Superadmin

Reserved for site owners and senior custodians of the archive. Superadmins oversee all operations and bear responsibility for the integrity and long-term preservation of the entire family dataset.

To be noticed for a Contributor badge, raise corrections or share family data using the archive tools. Sustained and accurate contributions are reviewed by admins. For Moderator or Admin roles, use the Apply button below.

How role requests are considered

Apply only if you have meaningful knowledge of the community, a sincere interest in learning more, and enough time to help carefully. Requests are reviewed by the site owners. Approval is not automatic, and the owners may approve, decline, pause, or remove access whenever needed to protect the archive.

A moderator or admin who does not log in for one continuous month may lose that role. This keeps sensitive access limited to people who are actively helping.

User Guide

This archive is private and community maintained. Use it slowly, verify what you find, and send corrections when you see missing or doubtful information.

First visit

Sign in, complete the registration and consent form, then search for your own profile. Claim only your own profile so admins can privately connect your email and mobile number with the correct record.

Finding family

Search by name, place, or badge. The search bar looks across names, birth place, current place, root place, and death place simultaneously. Open a profile to view details, then use Browse Tree to understand parents, spouses, and children around that profile.

How to search effectively

  • The search bar looks across names, birth place, current place, root place, and death place at the same time — type a town or village name to find everyone connected to it.
  • Type a badge name — contributor, moderator, admin, or superadmin — to instantly filter results to that group.
  • Try spelling variations and short name fragments for names you are unsure of.
  • Fuzzy search is on by default. It looks for spelling variants and common transliteration differences, such as similar sounds or alternate spellings.
  • If fuzzy search produces too many unrelated names, turn it off. Exact matching is better when you know the spelling or want tighter results.
  • Use the Filter drawer to narrow by gender, badge type, location group, or source family.
  • Use source-family labels to understand which GEDCOM file a profile came from.
  • If a name is missing, use Add / Correct Family rather than creating duplicate assumptions.

Reporting duplicate profiles

If two profiles look like the same person, open one profile, click Share to copy its profile URL, then paste that URL into the Profile Duplicacy section while raising a correction from the other profile.

Corrections and missing people

Use Add / Correct Family for spelling issues, place corrections, missing dates, missing relatives, duplicate profiles, and requests to add a person who is not yet represented. For duplicate profiles, use the Profile Duplicacy chevron and paste the copied profile URL from the Share button, plus a short note explaining why the two records seem to be the same person. Include enough context for an admin to verify the request: names, places, approximate dates, and known family links.

Profile claims

Claiming a profile is for your own profile only. Ancestors and shared relatives should not be claimed as personal profiles. If an ancestor is relevant to your line, mention that in corrections or contribution notes so admins can review the lineage context. Registered users should claim their own profile within 72 hours; accounts that do not claim in time may be blocked from signing in until admins review them.

Mobile number privacy

To protect the privacy and security of individuals and families listed in the Shajra, mobile numbers are not displayed publicly on the website.

While mobile numbers may be collected and securely stored in our backend systems for administrative, verification, moderation, and record-management purposes, they remain accessible only to authorised administrators and are never exposed to public users.

This policy has been adopted to minimise the risk of misuse, unsolicited contact, spam, fraud, data scraping, and other privacy concerns. The primary purpose of Apnon Ki Talash and the Iraqi Biradari Shajra is to help individuals understand family connections, ancestry, and lineage relationships. It is not intended to serve as a public directory for contacting or socialising with individuals listed in the family tree.

Date of birth privacy

Where available, dates of birth may be collected and stored within our records to improve the accuracy of genealogy data and family relationships.

However, dates of birth are not displayed publicly on the website. This information is retained only for administrative and record-keeping purposes and is accessible solely to authorised administrators where required.

This policy helps protect individuals from identity-related risks, privacy violations, unauthorised profiling, and misuse of personal information. The objective of the Shajra is to provide a meaningful understanding of family history and connections while maintaining appropriate safeguards for personal information and respecting the privacy of living individuals.

How to print a family tree

Step 1 — Find your focus person in Search. Go to the Search tab and search for the person you want to be the root of the printed tree — ideally the oldest ancestor whose family line you want to capture. Open their profile by clicking their name.

Step 2 — Click Browse Tree from their profile. On the profile page, click the 🌳 Browse Tree button. This takes you directly to the Browse Tree tab with that person already set as the focus. You will see the label "Showing tree for: [Name]" confirming who the tree is built around.

Step 3 — Wait for the tree to build. The tree loads all ancestors above and descendants below the focus person. Depending on the size of the family, this can take 3 to 5 minutes to fully render. Please be patient and do not refresh the page.

Step 4 — Expand generations. Once loaded, each family shows a ▼ N children toggle. Click it to expand and reveal that generation's children. Expand as many levels as you want to appear in your printout.

Step 5 — Click 🖨 Print Tree. When the tree shows the generations you need, click Print Tree. Only the tree currently visible on screen is printed. Use landscape orientation in your printer or PDF settings for best results.

Limitations: The tree displays a maximum of 6 ancestor generations upward and 6 descendant generations downward from the focus person. If your family spans more, choose a different focus person from the middle of the line to capture that section and print separately. The tree follows the paternal line upward — wives appear as spouses at each level but their own ancestry is not traversed.

Contacting site admins

For access problems, incorrect records, blocked accounts, urgent privacy concerns, or large shajra datasets, use Add / Correct Family with clear details. If you want to help review data regularly, use the Contribution tab to apply for a moderator or admin role.
