Support This Project

Help Keep the Archive Alive

Apno Ki Talash is a volunteer-run genealogy archive documenting the family histories of Shaikh families from Lar, Nawanagar, and across eastern Uttar Pradesh. It is free to use — no subscription, no paywalls. Your contribution helps cover server costs, keeps the search running, and supports ongoing archival work.

Where your contribution goes

  • Hosting and database costs for the genealogy archive
  • Ongoing research, data entry, and GEDCOM digitisation
  • Family tree corrections, new connections, and source verification
  • Keeping the service free for every family member worldwide

This is a non-commercial community project. Contributions are voluntary and go entirely toward the project. Even ₹50 makes a difference.
