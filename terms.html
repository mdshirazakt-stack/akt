1. Purpose of the Website

Apno Ki Talash exists to help individuals rediscover ancestors, extended family members, lost relatives, lineage, migration history, ancestral roots, and historical family connections.

The website is for cultural preservation, historical exploration, educational understanding, genealogical research, and community interest. It is not intended to establish legal identity, inheritance rights, caste claims, ownership rights, or official family status.

2. Nature and Source of Information

Information may come from oral traditions, folklore, family recollections, handwritten records, old archives, personal notes, historical references, previously circulated genealogical compilations, and community contributions.

A substantial portion is an English translation and digital compilation derived from the historical work “Apnon Ki Talash”, originally compiled by Jaleel Daroga during the 1950s. Additional information may be contributed by descendants, volunteers, researchers, historians, and community members.

3. No Claim of Historical, Scientific, or Legal Authenticity

Lineage records, family trees, names, dates, relationships, and historical references on this website are not certified legal records, government-authenticated genealogical documents, or fully verified historical or scientific records.

The content may contain inaccuracies, omissions, conflicting narratives, or incomplete records. Users should independently verify information before relying on it for official, legal, governmental, immigration, matrimonial, inheritance, property-related, or personal decisions.

4. Community-Driven and Enthusiast Initiative

This project is a collective effort by genealogy enthusiasts, translators, historians, researchers, archivists, and community contributors who believe in preserving ancestral memories and family narratives.

It should be viewed as a cultural preservation initiative, a community-driven genealogical archive, and an enthusiast-led documentation effort rather than an officially certified archive.
