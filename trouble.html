Help & Support

Trouble signing in?

If you are unable to access Apno Ki Talash, one of the following situations may apply. Read through each case below to understand what may have happened and how to resolve it.

⏰ Profile not claimed within 72 hours — account blocked

When you register, you are required to find and claim your own family profile within 72 hours. This step is essential to confirm that you are a genuine member of the family community. If this deadline passes without a claim being made, your account may be automatically blocked to maintain the integrity of the archive.

🚫 Account blocked by admin

An administrator may have manually blocked your account. This can happen if there was a concern about incorrect information submitted during registration, misuse of the archive, or suspicious activity. Blocked accounts cannot sign in until the block is reviewed and lifted by an admin.

❌ Signup request rejected

Your sign-up request may have been reviewed and rejected by an admin — for example, if your identity or family connection could not be verified against existing records in the archive. Rejected accounts cannot log in. If you believe this is an error, please contact us on WhatsApp below with your full name and email address.

🔄 Profile claim revoked — re-registration required

If an admin found that you claimed the wrong profile or submitted incorrect registration details, your claim may have been revoked and your account reset. You will need to sign in again, complete the registration form afresh with accurate information, and then find and claim your correct family profile. If you are unsure which profile is yours, contact us before claiming.

📱

Still need help? Contact us on WhatsApp

Send us a message with your full name, email address, and a brief description of the issue you are facing. We will review your case and get back to you as soon as possible.
